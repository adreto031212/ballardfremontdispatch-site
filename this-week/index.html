This week in Ballard & Fremont.

The dated things to know between now and next Thursday, from issue 1 of the Ballard & Fremont Dispatch. Every item links its public source.

Heads up

  • No school at elementary and K-8 schools, Monday to Wednesday, November 23 to 25. Seattle Public Schools holds family-teacher conferences those days; some middle schools also close for one of them, so check your school's site. Every school is closed Thursday and Friday, November 26 and 27. Seattle Public Schools calendar
  • 300 apartments proposed at 6400 8th Ave NW: comments through Wednesday, December 2. The city's record for the rezone, project 3044422-LU, lists the extended comment period ending that day; quote the project number when you comment. More below. Seattle permit record

What the city decided and what changed

Route 40's street work is finished through Ballard and Fremont

SDOT says major construction is done, with a ribbon cutting on October 29; crews may return for smaller tasks in the coming weeks.

A rezone at 6400 8th Ave NW would allow a six-story building with about 300 apartments

The application, filed October 26, asks for a contract rezone of the site from a 40-foot commercial zone and lowrise zoning to a 65-foot limit, for apartments, retail and 274 parking spaces below…

Ballard light rail's draft environmental study comes out December 11

Sound Transit opens a public comment period that day, running through February 9, with a hearing at the Nordic Museum on January 12.
