the drain on your corner

November 19, 2026 · Ballard & Fremont Dispatch, issue 1 · All Ballard & Fremont news

Fallen leaves are the first thing to clog a storm drain, and a clogged drain floods the street around it in a hard rain. Seattle Public Utilities' Adopt-a-Drain asks for fifteen minutes a month: pick a drain near your home, keep its grate clear of leaves and trash, and log what you collect in the program's online tool. As of October 2024, 2,044 volunteers had adopted 3,372 drains and collected more than 40,000 pounds of debris. Seattle Public Utilities
