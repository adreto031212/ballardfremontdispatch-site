Who we are and how we work.

Who publishes the Ballard & Fremont Dispatch

Sponsors

Every paid placement carries the word "Sponsored". Ads never buy coverage. No real estate brokerages or agents and no political ads as sponsors.

Market numbers

Sold homes and market numbers come from the Northwest Multiple Listing Service and public records, with the source named.

Your information

Your email, neighborhood and address (if you add one) stay with the Ballard & Fremont Dispatch. The sign-up form has a separate, unticked box: tick it and your neighborhood and address are shared with a local real estate brokerage under the same ownership as our publisher, which may contact you about your home. Leave it empty and nothing is shared, and you get every issue just the same. To change your mind later, reply to any issue. Nothing is sold, and advertisers never get reader emails. Unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Corrections

Tell us by replying to any issue or writing to hello@ballardfremontdispatch.com. Corrections run in the next issue and on the web copy within a day.
